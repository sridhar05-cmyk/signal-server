Strategy Engine & Technical Confluence Rules

RESEARCH SPECIFICATION
Trend Filter (Macro)
Long entries permitted only when 4h 200 EMA is sloping upward. Short entries permitted only when sloping downward. Counter-trend trades strictly filtered.
Confluence Engine (3 of 4)
  • Trend: EMA9 > EMA21 (Bull) / EMA9 < EMA21 (Bear)
  • Momentum: RSI in 40–65 pullbacks
  • MACD: Histogram turning positive/negative
  • Volatility: Pullback near EMA9 / Bands
Dynamic Trailing Exit
  • 1x ATR Profit: Move Stop-Loss to Breakeven
  • 2x ATR Profit: Trail Stop 1x ATR behind price
  • Risk Cap: Fixed 2% risk, NO martingale
Empirical Confidence Invariant
Results require minimum N ≥ 30 sample size. Any regime or pair with N < 30 is labeled Insufficient Data with win rates withheld to prevent estimation bias.

Transparency Standard: All metrics represent empirical walk-forward backtest simulations over authentic historical market data (Binance Public API for Crypto, Yahoo Finance for Spot Forex). This standalone console is strictly for offline research observation and does not place trades or connect to any brokerage execution API.
